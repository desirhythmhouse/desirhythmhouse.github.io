Privacy Policy

Desi Rhythm House — Last updated: October 25, 2026

Desi Rhythm House is an Indian wedding and party instrumental music channel on YouTube. This policy describes our internal publishing tool, "Desi Rhythm House Publisher", which posts our own content to our own accounts on Pinterest, Instagram and Facebook.

What the tool does

  • Creates Pins on our own Pinterest boards for each new music mix we publish on YouTube, using the video's thumbnail, a short description and a link to the video.
  • Publishes our own short music videos (Reels) to our own Instagram account and Facebook Page.

Information we access

The tool only accesses the accounts owned and operated by Desi Rhythm House. It uses the access tokens issued to us by Pinterest and Meta solely to create and read our own Pins, boards and posts, and to read basic performance statistics of our own content.

We do not access, collect, store, sell or share any information about other users, followers or viewers.

Data storage

Access tokens for our own accounts are stored securely on a private computer we operate and are never shared with third parties. We keep a simple log of what we posted (post IDs and dates) to avoid duplicate posts. No personal data of any user is stored.

Third-party services

Our content is published on YouTube, Pinterest, Instagram and Facebook. Your use of those platforms is governed by their own privacy policies.

Changes

We may update this policy from time to time. The latest version will always be available on this page.

Contact

For questions about this policy, please contact us through our YouTube channel: youtube.com/@desirhythmhouseofficial.
